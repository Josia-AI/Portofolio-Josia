01 / OVERVIEW

Find the right wear-resistant steel, faster.Cari material wear-resistant steel dengan lebih cepat.

The TSG screenshots show a material-supplier homepage, a multi-category industrial catalog (wear-resistant plate, stainless steel, carbon steel, pipe, round bar, valves, fittings, and structures), company information, and a WhatsApp sales quotation modal. Hardox, Abrex, Everhard, and NM form part of the intended wear-plate product scope.Screenshot TSG menunjukkan beranda pemasok material, katalog berbagai kategori (wear-resistant plate, stainless steel, carbon steel, pipa, round bar, valve, fitting, dan steel structure), informasi perusahaan, serta popup kontak sales quotation WhatsApp. Hardox, Abrex, Everhard, dan seri NM merupakan bagian dari cakupan produk wear plate yang direncanakan.
