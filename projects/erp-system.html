01 / OVERVIEW

One workflow from quotation to operational reporting.Satu alur dari penawaran hingga pelaporan operasional.

ERP SYSTEM GLOBAL brings the visible interfaces for quotation/penawaran, invoices, delivery orders, purchase orders, item/customer/supplier master data, user access levels, reporting, and company profile settings together in one dashboard. The screenshots document the application interface; an end-to-end backend audit is outside the scope of this showcase.ERP SYSTEM GLOBAL menyajikan antarmuka quotation/penawaran, invoice, surat jalan, purchase order, master barang, customer dan supplier, pengaturan akses pengguna, laporan, serta profil perusahaan dalam satu dashboard. Screenshot memperlihatkan tampilan aplikasinya; pengujian backend menyeluruh tidak termasuk dalam portofolio ini.
